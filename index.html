How to study

  1. Diagnose first. Answer each module's opening question without notes.
  2. Short blocks. About 25 minutes a day, one module every two days.
  3. Teach it back in your own words, then work the scenario.
  4. Quiz one question at a time before checking.
  5. Re-test each module 1, 3, 7 and 21 days after you finish it.
