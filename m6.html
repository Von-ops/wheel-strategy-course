Module 6: Paper drill and the go-live gate

Diagnose first. Before reading: could you explain a trade to a colleague without opening your broker app? If not, stay on paper.

Your broker permissions

Covered calls and cash-secured puts need only basic (Level 2) options approval in a cash account. Spreads need Level 3 and a margin account, and this course does not use them.

Execution basics

Use limit orders near the middle of the bid and ask. Favor options with tight spreads and healthy volume and open interest. Stocks and options settle the next trading day.

The six-field drill

Before any real trade, state: strike, expiration, collateral or share cover, max loss in dollars, your plan if assigned, and your stop. A blank or conflicting field means no trade.

Go live slowly

Do at least three paper trades first, including one where you walk through a bad outcome. Then start with one contract and review every fill.

Worked example

Illustrative drill: "One XYZ $50 put, expiring in 30 days. Collateral $5,000. Worst case $4,900. If assigned, I sell a call at $52 or above. My exit: buy back if the put doubles in price. It fits my caps and cash floor because…"

Applied scenario
