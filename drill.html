Go-live checklist

  • Existing positions within your caps
  • Settled cash available without touching the cash floor
  • Worst-case loss fits the 1–2% rule
  • No earnings or big macro event inside the window
  • Strike reviewed; spread and volume checked
  • Three paper drills done, including a bad outcome
  • Fill logged and reviewed after the trade
