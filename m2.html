Module 2: Covered calls

Diagnose first. Before reading: if your call is assigned, exactly what happens to your shares? Are you truly OK selling them at the strike?

The mechanics

One covered call is backed by 100 shares you already own. You sell to open the call and collect the premium. If the stock is above the strike at expiration, your shares are usually sold at the strike. If it is below, the call expires and you keep the shares and the premium. You can buy to close before then to end the promise.

Choosing the strike

Pick a strike you would be glad to sell at, not just the one with the biggest premium. The course rule is 0.20–0.30 delta and 21–45 days to expiration. Lower delta means more room for the stock to rise but less premium. Avoid selling the day before earnings, the first 30–60 minutes and last 30 minutes of the trading day, and around CPI, Fed and jobs reports.

Managing it

Plan to buy the call back once you have captured 50–70% of the premium. That frees the shares to sell a new call and cuts the risk of being caught by a late rally. Premium income is not the same as total profit: the shares can still lose value.

What a covered call does not do

It does not reduce how concentrated you are in a stock. You still own all the shares until they are actually sold. Only cover shares you have; long calls are not cover in a cash account.

Worked example

Illustrative: you own 100 shares of XYZ at $100. You sell 1 call at the $110 strike, 35 days out, for $2.00 a share, collecting $200. Breakeven from today is $98. If XYZ ends at $115, you sell at $110 and keep the $200, for a total gain of $1,200, but you miss the move from $110 to $115. If you buy the call back for $0.80, you lock in $120 of premium profit (60%).

Teach it back

Explain the difference between premium income and total profit. Then say: "I could sell these shares at the strike, and here is why that is or is not acceptable."

Applied scenario

XYZ jumps to $125 right before expiration. Your shares get sold at $110. Write down why you will not chase it by paying up to roll, and what you will do with the cash.
